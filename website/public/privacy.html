Privacy

Last updated 16 October 2026.

The app

Sirus Code runs on your Mac. It has no account and sends nothing to us. Your projects, conversations and settings stay in one database on your computer.

The agent CLIs you connect (Claude Code, Codex and others) send your prompts and code to their own providers under their own terms. Sirus does not add anything to that.

Connectors

When you sign in to a connector, its token is stored in your Mac's Keychain and sent only to that service.

The iPhone app

The iPhone app talks directly to your Mac or server over your Tailscale network. Nothing passes through a server of ours.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It is served by Cloudflare, which may keep standard request logs.

Contact

Questions go to the issue tracker on GitHub.
